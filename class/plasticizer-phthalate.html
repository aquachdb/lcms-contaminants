Phthalate plasticizers

72 ionsm/z 121.0284 – 819.517267 positive5 negative

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS.
