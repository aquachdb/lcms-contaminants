Non-ionic surfactants

1875 ionsm/z 185.1148 – 1948.37041175 positive700 negative

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed.
