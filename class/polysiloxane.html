Siloxanes and silicones

153 ionsm/z 73.0115 – 1311.5758116 positive37 negative

Cyclic (D4-D7) and linear dimethylsiloxanes spaced 74.0188 apart, from vial septa, tubing, seals, column bleed and ambient laboratory air. Their silicon isotope envelope (M+2 about 10-25%) is the giveaway.
