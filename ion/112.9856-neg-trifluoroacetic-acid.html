At m/z 112.9856 in negative ion mode, this is [M-H]- of Trifluoroacetic acid (C2HF3O2, monoisotopic 113.9929).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Ion-pairing reagents, Salt clusters.

Where it comes from

Ion-pairing acid modifier; persistent LC system contamination.

Other sources report the same ion from: 0.05-0.1 percent TFA ion-pairing modifier for peptides/proteins; High-mass ESI calibrant; Sodium plus residual TFA.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.9, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →

How to recognise it

  • Trifluoroacetate; extremely strong ESI signal suppressor in positive mode; persists in the system for days and forms [M+TFA-H]- adducts on analytes
  • [(CF3COONa)n+CF3COO]- calibrant series; repeat mass 135.9748 Da; practical n=0-12; the standard negative-mode TOF calibrant
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 112.9855876
