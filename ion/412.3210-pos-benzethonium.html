Where it comes from

Antiseptic and preservative in reagents, disinfectant residue.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.7, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

Contains a PEG-like ether tail but is a discrete quat; fragment 91.0542
