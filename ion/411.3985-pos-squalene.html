Where it comes from

Skin and sebum transfer from fingers, hair, ambient dust.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic, often at column wash.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 11.6, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

Dominant fingerprint marker; strong in APCI+ and ESI+ with ammonium; fragments 81.0699/95.0855/109.1012 isoprenoid series; reduce by nitrile gloves…
