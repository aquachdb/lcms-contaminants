Where it comes from

Flame retardant and plasticizer in plastics, PVC, electronics.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.6, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →
