Where it comes from

Hand cream, conditioner and cosmetic emulsifier transfer.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.3, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

Poor ESI response; usually as sodiated or water-loss ion; better in APCI+
