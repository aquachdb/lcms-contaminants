Where it comes from

Plasma, serum and urine matrix.

Other sources report the same ion from: Sweat and fingerprint transfer, urine carryover.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.8, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
