Where it comes from

Modern fabric softener (esterquat) transfer from lab coats and textiles.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

Biodegradable esterquat replacing DTDMAC; permanent cation ~666.6; hydrolyses to monoesterquat
