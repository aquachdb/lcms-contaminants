Where it comes from

Degradation of pentafluorophenyl (PFP/F5) bonded phases; also a residual reagent from PFP-ester chemistry.

Chromatography: elutes early.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Very acidic phenol, strong negative-mode responder with a distinctly negative mass defect. Its appearance on an F5 column indicates ring cleavage…
