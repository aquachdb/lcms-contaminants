Where it comes from

Wetting agent in labware coatings, excipient and cleaning formulations, dispersants.

Other sources report the same ion from: Anionic wetting agent (docusate) in cleaners and formulations.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

  • Extremely persistent negative-mode background; fragment 227 and 80.9646; sticks to LC tubing and PEEK
  • NORMAN-SLE S7 EAWAGSURF suspect 'DHSS'; anionic surfactant, detected as the deprotonated ion in ESI-
