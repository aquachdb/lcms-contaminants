Where it comes from

Nitro musk fragrance in soaps and detergents.

Other sources report the same ion from: Artificial sweetener; Handling and water background.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.7, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

  • Nitro musk; ESI- often better than ESI+ due to nitro groups
  • ESI+ 295.1288 fragment 180.102; ESI- 293.1143
