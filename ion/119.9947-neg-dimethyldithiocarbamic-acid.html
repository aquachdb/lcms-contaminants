Where it comes from

Degradation product of thiuram and zinc dithiocarbamate rubber accelerators.

Chromatography: void volume to early eluting.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.9, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →
