At m/z 158.9640 in positive ion mode, this is [M+Na]+ of Sodium trifluoroacetate cluster (C2F3NaO2, monoisotopic 135.9748).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.

Where it comes from

TFA-containing mobile phase with sodium; also used as calibrant.

Other sources report the same ion from: High-mass ESI calibrant; Also sodium plus residual TFA in the system; Salt.

Chromatography: void volume.

Sodium formate, sodium acetate, sodium trifluoroacetate, caesium and sodium iodide ladders. Used as calibrants on purpose and seen by accident whenever salt reaches the source. More salt clusters →
