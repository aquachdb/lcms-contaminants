Where it comes from

Residue from Wittig/Mitsunobu synthesis, plastics and reagent-grade solvents; extremely common ESI+ background.

Other sources report the same ion from: Residue from Wittig/Mitsunobu/Pd-catalysed synthesis, plastic additive; Crystallizing agent; Wittig/Mitsunobu synthesis byproduct.

Chromatography: elutes late reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.8, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ions that are commonly seen and reliably reported but do not belong to any of the other classes. More miscellaneous background ions →

How to recognise it

  • Extremely persistent ESI+ background ion, very stable, contaminates ion sources for weeks
  • Classic 279.0933 background ion, often used as tuning/lock mass; fragments 201.046 and 77.039; very hard to remove from an instrument once introduced
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 279.0933294
