Where it comes from

Limonene oxidation product, mint fragrance and flavor.

Other sources report the same ion from: Limonene oxidation and citrus fragrance.

Chromatography: elutes mid/late reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.4, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →
