Where it comes from

Common drug/handling background from fingers, dust, shared lab surfaces and tap water.

Other sources report the same ion from: Paraben hydrolysis product in aged reagents and buffers.

Chromatography: elutes early/mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.3, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
