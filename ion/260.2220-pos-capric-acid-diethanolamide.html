Where it comes from

Shampoo/detergent thickener and foam booster, hand soap residue on glassware, personal care carryover.

Other sources report the same ion from: Cocamide DEA foam booster in soaps and cleaners.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late gradient, later with longer acyl chain.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.4, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionize extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognize it

  • Fatty acid alkanolamide homolog series; members differ by CH2 (14.0157) and by 2H (2.0157) for unsaturation. Commercial cocamide DEA is a mixture…
  • NORMAN-SLE S7 EAWAGSURF suspect 'C9DEA'; nonionic surfactant; ESI+ response is usually dominated by [M+NH4]+ and [M+Na]+ rather than [M+H]+
