Where it comes from

HALS degradation/hydrolysis product from polyolefins and coatings.

Other sources report the same ion from: Degradation of hindered amine light stabilizers in polypropylene labware.

Chromatography: void volume to early eluting.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.9, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

  • Very basic, elutes near the void on reversed phase; strong ESI+ response
  • Hydrolysis/degradation fragment of Tinuvin-type stabilizers; basic and highly ESI-responsive
