Where it comes from

Plastic tubing, vial caps and septa, gloves, laboratory dust.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastic tubing, vial caps and septa, adhesives, gloves.

Chromatography: elutes late on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognizable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

  • Ammonium adduct with ammonium formate or acetate
  • Ammonium adduct with ammonium-containing eluents
