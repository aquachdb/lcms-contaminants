Where it comes from

Dimethyl phthalate in plastics and cosmetics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastic vials, tubing, solvent bottle caps, ink and coating residues; PET manufacturing residue, packaging; Plasticiser.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.6, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Fragments to m/z 163.0390 (loss of methanol) and the universal phthalate ion m/z 149.0233
  • Isomeric with dimethyl phthalate; fragments to m/z 163.0390
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 195.0651864
