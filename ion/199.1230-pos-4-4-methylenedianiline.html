Where it comes from

Polyurethane and epoxy hydrolysis, MDI residue in PU tubing and foams.

Chromatography: elutes early to mid.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.6, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it

Primary aromatic amine marker for PU/MDI materials; very strong ESI+
