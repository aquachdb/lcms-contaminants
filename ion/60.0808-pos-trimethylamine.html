Where it comes from

Degradation of choline/betaine, amine reagents, and ambient background.

Other sources report the same ion from: In-source degradation of quaternary ammonium and sulfobetaine ligands from CSH, SAX and zwitterionic HILIC….

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

TFA, HFBA, triethylamine, hexylamine and related reagents, which persist in an LC system for a very long time after the method that used them. More ion-pairing reagents →

How to recognise it

  • Protonated trimethylamine; ubiquitous low-mass positive background
  • Hofmann-type breakdown product of any trialkylammonium-bearing ligand; at 60.0808 it sits below most scan ranges and is a general amine background,…
