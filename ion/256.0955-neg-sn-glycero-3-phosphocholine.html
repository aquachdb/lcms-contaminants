Where it comes from

Zwitterionic phosphorylcholine HILIC phases (ZIC-cHILIC) and phospholipid carryover.

Chromatography: retained in HILIC segment.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -2.3, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it
