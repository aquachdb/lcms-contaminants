Where it comes from

Plastic tubing, pharmaceutical coatings.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasticiser; Plasticizer in PVC and coatings; Fatty acid from skin, gloves and plastic lubricants.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →
