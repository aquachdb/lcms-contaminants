At m/z 215.0328 in negative ion mode, this is [M+Cl]- of D-Glucose (C6H12O6, monoisotopic 180.0634).

Where it comes from

Cell culture media, plasma matrix.

Other sources report the same ion from: Cornstarch glove powder, paper and general handling residue.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • From powdered gloves and starch; ESI- as [M+Cl]- 215.0328 or [M-H]- 179.0561; poorly retained on C18
  • Chloride adduct is the usual negative-mode form for neutral sugars
