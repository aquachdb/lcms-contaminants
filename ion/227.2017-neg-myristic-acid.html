Where it comes from

Skin/sebum transfer, soaps and detergents.

Other sources report the same ion from: Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Chromatography: elutes mid/late reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
