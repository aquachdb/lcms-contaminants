Where it comes from

Disulfide reduction before alkylation and digestion.

Other sources report the same ion from: Air oxidation of beta-mercaptoethanol.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.4, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Derivatisation reagents, chaotropes, reducing and alkylating agents, and extraction additives. More sample-preparation reagents →

How to recognise it

  • Poor proton affinity so weak in positive mode; watch for the isobaric 2-mercaptoethanol disulfide dimer with the same formula
  • EXACTLY isobaric with DTT (same formula C4H10O2S2); distinguish by MS2 and by which reductant was used
