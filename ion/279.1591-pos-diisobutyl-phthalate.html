Where it comes from

Plastic tubing, vials, gloves, caps.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastic tubing, vial caps and septa, gloves, laboratory dust; Plastic tubing, vial caps and septa, adhesives, gloves; DEHP hydrolysis product and CID fragment, PVC leachate.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Isomeric with DBP and with mono(2-ethylhexyl) phthalate; diagnostic fragment m/z 149.0233
  • Ubiquitous background; CID gives m/z 205.0859 then 149.0233; chromatographically resolved from DiBP
  • Also observed as the dominant CID fragment of DEHP [M+H]+ and [M+NH4]+; isomeric with DBP/DiBP
