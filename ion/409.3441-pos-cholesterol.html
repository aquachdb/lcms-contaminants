At m/z 409.3441 in positive ion mode, this is [M+Na]+ of Cholesterol (C27H46O, monoisotopic 386.3549).

Where it comes from

Skin and sebum transfer, serum carryover, dust.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasma, serum, FBS in media; Cholesterol-bonded phases (Cosmosil Cholester, Ascentis Cholesterol) after ester or ether cleavage; Also ubiquitous biological background.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.7, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognise it

  • ESI+ almost always as water-loss ion 369.3516; ammoniated 404.3887 with ammonium acetate; APCI+ preferred
  • Sodiated cholesterol
  • Released cholesteryl head group. Protonated cholesterol is weak because it dehydrates almost completely in source, so the ammonium and sodium adducts…
