Where it comes from

Plastic labware and tubing.

Other sources report the same ion from: PVC, adhesives, laboratory plastics; DIDP degradation and CID fragment.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.8, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Fragment m/z 149.0233; isomeric with monoisodecyl phthalate
  • Signature CID fragment of DIDP; branched isomer mixture
  • Phthalate ester plasticiser; yields m/z 149 fragment; Di-n-pentylphthalate
