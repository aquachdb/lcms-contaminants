At m/z 214.0896 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of N-Butylbenzenesulfonamide (C10H15NO2S, monoisotopic 213.0823).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Labware leachables, Slip agents and fatty amides.

Where it comes from

Nylon plasticizer leaching from filters, frits and tubing.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasticizer for nylon; Leaches from nylon filters, frits, tubing and cable ties; Plasticiser.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.1, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Classic nylon-component leachate accompanying caprolactam and nylon cyclic oligomers; fragment 141.0005 and 77.0386
