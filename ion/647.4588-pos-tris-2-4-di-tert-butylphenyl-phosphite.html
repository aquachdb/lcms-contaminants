Where it comes from

Other sources report the same ion from: Secondary phosphite antioxidant in polyolefin labware, tips, tubes, films; Extractable/leachable from single-use polymeric films and bioprocess bags (LC-HRMS E&L screening).

Chromatography: standardized RT 17.38 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 15.5, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it

  • Very common polyolefin leachable; readily oxidises in-source and on storage to the phosphate at m/z 663.4539
  • From the Full MS/ddMS2 inclusion list used for E&L screening of single-use plastic film
