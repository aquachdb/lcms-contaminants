Where it comes from

PTFE and fluoropolymer tubing, seals and degasser membranes; system blank background.

Other sources report the same ion from: PFAS background from PTFE/fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottles, LC seals and reagents; PTFE and PFA frits, fluoropolymer tubing, fluoropolymer-lined bottles and filter membranes in the flow path; PFAS from PTFE tubing, frits, vial caps and solvents.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →
