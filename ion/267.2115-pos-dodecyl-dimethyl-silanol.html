Where it comes from

Dodecyl (C12) reversed-phase columns (Synergi Max-RP style C12 phases).

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognize it

Intermediate homolog of the alkylsilanol bleed series; the series is spaced by 14.0157 across C4/C8/C12/C16/C18/C30 phases
