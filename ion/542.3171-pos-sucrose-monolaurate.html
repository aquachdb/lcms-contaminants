Where it comes from

Mild non-ionic detergent, food-grade emulsifier carryover.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.5, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →
