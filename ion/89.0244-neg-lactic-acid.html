At m/z 89.0244 in negative ion mode, this is [M-H]- of Lactic acid (C3H6O3, monoisotopic 90.0317).

Check it on PubChem: CID 612 ↗

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Personal-care and cosmetic residues, Labware leachables.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

Sweat and fingerprint transfer, skin surface.

Other sources report the same ion from: Self-tanner active transfer from skin; Plasma, spent cell culture media; Polylactide / polylactic acid plasticware and detergents.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.7, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • 89.0244 in ESI-; poorly retained on C18; ubiquitous in sweat-contaminated blanks
  • Isomeric with lactic acid; poorly retained; weak ionization
  • Accumulates massively in spent media; suppresses early-eluting analytes
