Where it comes from

Bile salt detergent, membrane protein and lipase assay buffers.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasma, bile, intestinal and liver samples.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient on C18.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.2, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

  • Diagnostic taurine fragment 79.9574 (SO3-.) and 124.0074 (taurine sulfonate); strong negative-mode responder
  • Conjugated bile acid; strong negative-mode ion; MS2 gives the taurine fragment 79.957 and 124.007
