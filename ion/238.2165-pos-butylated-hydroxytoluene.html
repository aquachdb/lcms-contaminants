Where it comes from

Stabiliser in THF/ethers; plastic leachable.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →

How to recognise it

Ammoniated BHT with ammonium-containing mobile phases
