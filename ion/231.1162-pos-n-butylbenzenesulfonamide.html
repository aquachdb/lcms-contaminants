Where it comes from

Plasticizer in nylon/polyamide tubing and filters.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasticiser; Plasticizer; Plasticizer in nylon/polyamide, tubing, filters.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.1, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
