Where it comes from

Rosin from paper, adhesives and labels.

Other sources report the same ion from: Rosin/colophony from adhesives, paper and gloves.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.6, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognize it

  • ESI- 299.2017; the more stable rosin acid seen in paper contact blanks
  • Detected in the procedural BLANK of the NORMAN collaborative trial (blank intensity 5427 vs sample 5284; RP-LC RT 13.9 min); DTXSID DTXSID8022163
