Where it comes from

Extractable/leachable from single-use polymeric films and bioprocess bags (LC-HRMS E&L screening).

Other sources report the same ion from: Nylon-6 filters, frits, syringe filters, tubing, membranes, packaging films; Nylon syringe filters and membranes, nylon frits and tubing; Nylon consumables (filters, mesh, tubing), skin and paper contact.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it

  • Extremely common early-eluting ESI+ background from nylon filters and membranes; base of the 113.0841 spaced oligomer comb
  • Hallmark nylon filter leachate; very polar and elutes near void; accompanied by cyclic dimer and trimer
  • ESI+ 114.0913; nylon-6 monomer, very common lab background; cyclic oligomer series belongs to the polymer table
