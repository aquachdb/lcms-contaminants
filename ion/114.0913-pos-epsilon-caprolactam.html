Where it comes from

Nylon syringe filters and membranes, nylon frits and tubing.

Other sources report the same ion from: Nylon-6 filters, frits, syringe filters, tubing, membranes, packaging films; Nylon consumables (filters, mesh, tubing), skin and paper contact; Nylon-6 monomer leaching from filters and frits.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognize it

  • Extremely common early-eluting ESI+ background from nylon filters and membranes; base of the 113.0841 spaced oligomer comb
  • Hallmark nylon filter leachate; very polar and elutes near void; accompanied by cyclic dimer and trimer
  • ESI+ 114.0913; nylon-6 monomer, very common lab background; cyclic oligomer series belongs to the polymer table
