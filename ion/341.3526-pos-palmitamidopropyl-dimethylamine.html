Where it comes from

Residual amidoamine impurity in cocamidopropyl betaine, shampoo and hand soap residue.

Chromatography: elutes mid gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.3, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

Amidoamine precursor/impurity of cocamidopropyl betaine; exactly 58.0055 Da below the betaine (loss of CH2COO). Homologs differ by CH2 (14.0157).…
