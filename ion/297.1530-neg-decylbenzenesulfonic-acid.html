Where it comes from

Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Other sources report the same ion from: Alconox and Liquinox type lab detergents, glassware washing residue; Detergent residue and water background; Anionic detergent in lab dishwashing and cleaning agents.

Chromatography: standardized RT 1.9 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.8, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →
