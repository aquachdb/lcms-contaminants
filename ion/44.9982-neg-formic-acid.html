At m/z 44.9982 in negative ion mode, this is [M-H]- of Formic acid (CH2O2, monoisotopic 46.0055).

Where it comes from

Mobile phase modifier.

Other sources report the same ion from: Oxidation of methanol/formaldehyde; Also an additive; 0.1 percent formic acid mobile-phase modifier.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.2, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →

How to recognise it

  • Formate background from methanol oxidation as well as from formic acid additive
  • Formate anion; the dominant low-mass ESI- background ion in FA mobile phases; also forms [M+HCOO]- adducts on neutral analytes
  • Negative-mode cluster [(NH4HCO2)n+HCO2]-; repeat mass 63.0320 Da; useful n=0-5
