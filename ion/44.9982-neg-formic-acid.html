Where it comes from

Oxidation of methanol/formaldehyde; also an additive.

Other sources report the same ion from: 0.1 percent formic acid mobile-phase modifier; 2-10 mM ammonium formate buffer; ESI calibration solution.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.2, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Impurities, stabilizers and degradation products carried in by the solvents themselves, plus their cluster ions. More solvent and mobile-phase impurities →

How to recognize it

  • Formate background from methanol oxidation as well as from formic acid additive
  • Formate anion; the dominant low-mass ESI- background ion in FA mobile phases; also forms [M+HCOO]- adducts on neutral analytes
  • Negative-mode cluster [(NH4HCO2)n+HCO2]-; repeat mass 63.0320 Da; useful n=0-5
