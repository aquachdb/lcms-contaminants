Where it comes from

ProClin and Kathon preservatives in antibody stocks, buffers and reagents.

Other sources report the same ion from: Cosmetic, shampoo and cleaning product preservative transfer.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.0, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Preservatives from reagents, buffers and consumables, including biocides in water systems. More antimicrobials and preservatives →

How to recognize it

  • Very polar; elutes near void; ProClin 300 is a CMIT/MIT blend used in immunoassay reagents
  • ESI+ 116.0165; poorly retained on C18
