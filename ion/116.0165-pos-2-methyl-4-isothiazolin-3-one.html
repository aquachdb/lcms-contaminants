Where it comes from

Other sources report the same ion from: Cosmetic, shampoo and cleaning product preservative transfer.

How to recognise it

  • Very polar; elutes near void; ProClin 300 is a CMIT/MIT blend used in immunoassay reagents
  • ESI+ 116.0165; poorly retained on C18
