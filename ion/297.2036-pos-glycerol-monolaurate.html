Where it comes from

Internal lubricant, antifog and antistatic additive in polyolefin labware and films.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.2, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognise it

Plastic additive leachate; also [M+NH4]+ 292.2482 and [M+H-H2O]+ 257.2111
