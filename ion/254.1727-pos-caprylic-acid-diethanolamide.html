Where it comes from

Shampoo/detergent thickener and foam booster, hand soap residue on glassware, personal care carryover.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late gradient, later with longer acyl chain.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognise it
