Where it comes from

Shampoo/detergent foam stabilizer, cosmetics, hand soap residue on glassware.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late gradient, later with longer acyl chain.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.0, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionize extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognize it

Fatty acid alkanolamide homolog series; members differ by CH2 (14.0157) and by 2H (2.0157) for unsaturation. Commercial cocamide MEA is a mixture…
