Where it comes from

Plastic tubing/vials, gloves.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastic tubing, vial caps and septa, gloves, laboratory dust; Plastic tubing, vial caps and septa, adhesives, gloves; Plasticiser.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognizable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

  • Sodium adduct dominates in low-additive mobile phases
  • Very common sodiated background ion in ESI+
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 301.1410314
