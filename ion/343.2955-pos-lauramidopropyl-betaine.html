Where it comes from

Shampoo and hand soap residue on glassware, personal care carryover, mild detergent in lab washing.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hand soap and lab cleaner residue on glassware; Hand soap, shampoo and body wash residue; Amphoteric surfactant in hand soap and shampoo.

Chromatography: elutes mid gradient; zwitterion so retention is pH sensitive.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.4, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

  • C12 homolog of a coconut-derived blend; comb at 315.26 (C10), 343.30 (C12), 371.33 (C14); classic hand-soap contamination
  • Zwitterion, ESI+ 343.2955; homolog comb spaced 28.0313; frequent hand-soap carryover
  • Zwitterionic amidopropyl betaine; homologs differ by CH2 (14.0157). Commercial CAPB is dominated by the C12 lauramidopropyl betaine (m/z 343.30) with…
