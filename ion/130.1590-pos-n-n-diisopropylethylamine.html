Where it comes from

Peptide synthesis base and oligonucleotide ion-pairing additive.

Other sources report the same ion from: Dibutylammonium acetate (DBAA) ion-pairing system for oligonucleotides; Ion-pairing reagent for acidic analytes; Ion-pairing reagent for oligonucleotide LC-MS.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.2, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

TFA, HFBA, triethylamine, hexylamine and related reagents, which persist in an LC system for a very long time after the method that used them. More ion-pairing reagents →

How to recognize it

  • Protonated DIPEA; a classic synthesis-derived carryover ion
  • Protonated dibutylamine; isomeric with DIPEA and octylamine at 130.1590
  • Protonated octylamine
