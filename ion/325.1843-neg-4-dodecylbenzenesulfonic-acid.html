Where it comes from

Alconox, Liquinox, Decon and other anionic lab detergents; glassware wash residue.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hand soap, laboratory detergent and municipal water background; Anionic detergent in lab dishwashing and cleaning agents; Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

  • Dominant LAS homolog and the classic detergent-residue ion; several isomer peaks; fragment 183.0116 and 80.9646
  • LAS homolog comb 297/311/325/339 in ESI-; fragment 183.011 and 80.9646
  • NORMAN-SLE S7 EAWAGSURF suspect 'C12-LAS'; anionic surfactant, detected as the deprotonated ion in ESI-
