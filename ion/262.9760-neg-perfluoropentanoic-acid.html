Where it comes from

PFAS background from PTFE/fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottles, LC seals and reagents.

Other sources report the same ion from: LC system PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottle caps and SPE consumables; Short-chain PFAS target of EPA Method 533.

Chromatography: elutes mid/late reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.9, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognize it

  • ESI- only; fragment 218.98; mitigate with a PFAS delay column between pump and injector; homolog comb spaced CF2 49.9968
  • EPA Method 533 analyte (short-chain/ether PFAS, C4-C12). Listed in the EPA method comparison table with CASRN 2706-90-3 but no m/z is given in that…
