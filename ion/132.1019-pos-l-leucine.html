At m/z 132.1019 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of L-Leucine (C6H13NO2, monoisotopic 131.0946).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Other polymer oligomers.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

Cell culture media, plasma matrix.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hydrolysis of nylon-6 filters and membranes.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.5, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • Very polar, elutes at the void on reversed phase; strong ESI+
  • Leu and Ile are isobaric; MS2 gives 86.096
