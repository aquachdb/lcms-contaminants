Where it comes from

Cationic vesicle and transfection reagents, disinfectant residue.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 11.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →
