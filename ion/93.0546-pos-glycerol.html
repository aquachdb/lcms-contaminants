Where it comes from

Enzyme/protein storage buffers (50 percent glycerol), cryoprotection.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.8, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Derivatisation reagents, chaotropes, reducing and alkylating agents, and extraction additives. More sample-preparation reagents →

How to recognise it

Enzymes and antibodies are stored in 50 percent glycerol so it enters every digest; huge void-volume peak and a severe suppressor
