Where it comes from

Secondary phosphite antioxidant in polyolefin labware, tips, tubes, films.

Other sources report the same ion from: Extractable/leachable from single-use polymeric films and bioprocess bags (LC-HRMS E&L screening); Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 15.5, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

  • Very common polyolefin leachable; readily oxidises in-source and on storage to the phosphate at m/z 663.4539
  • From the Full MS/ddMS2 inclusion list used for E&L screening of single-use plastic film
