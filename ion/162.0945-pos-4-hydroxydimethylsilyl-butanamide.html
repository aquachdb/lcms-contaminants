Where it comes from

Aged cyano/nitrile (CN) columns run in acidic aqueous mobile phase.

Chromatography: elutes early.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Nitrile hydration product; its appearance is the chemical signature that a CN column has been hydrolysing and is losing selectivity
