Where it comes from

C30 carotenoid/isomer phases (YMC Carotenoid C30, Acclaim C30, Develosil C30).

Chromatography: elutes very late/highly lipophilic.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognize it

C30 phases are usually polymerically bonded and are run hot, so ligand bleed is more noticeable than on C18; elutes right at the end of the gradient…
