Where it comes from

Hindered amine light stabiliser in polyolefins, films, plastic labware.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.5, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →
