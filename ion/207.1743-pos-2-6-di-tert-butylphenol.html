Where it comes from

Phenolic antioxidant/degradant from plastics and detergents.

Other sources report the same ion from: Degradation product of phosphite antioxidants, plastic labware; Triton X degradation, plastic and rubber additive; Antioxidant degradation product from polypropylene labware.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →

How to recognise it

  • Ionises poorly in ESI+; better in APCI+ or as [M-H]- in ESI-
  • Weak in ESI+
  • Weak in positive mode; negative mode preferred
