Where it comes from

Hydrolysis product of PMSF in aqueous lysis buffer.

Other sources report the same ion from: Aryl SCX and mixed-mode phases; Also a tosylate counter-ion and a residual sulfonation reagent.

Chromatography: elutes early-mid.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
