Where it comes from

Epoxidised soybean oil (ESBO) in PVC gaskets and closures.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.2, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

Triepoxide from linolenate; minor ESBO marker
