Where it comes from

Silicone oil, silicone tubing and septa, syringe barrel lubricant, defoamers, vacuum grease.

Other sources report the same ion from: Condensation of trimethylsilyl endcapping residue from silica columns; Also silicone grease and septa.

Chromatography: void volume to early eluting.

Cyclic (D4-D7) and linear dimethylsiloxanes spaced 74.0188 apart, from vial septa, tubing, seals, column bleed and ambient laboratory air. Their silicon isotope envelope (M+2 about 10-25%) is the giveaway. More siloxanes and silicones →

How to recognize it

  • Smallest linear PDMS background species; dimethylsiloxane repeat spacing 74.0188 between consecutive oligomers
