Where it comes from

Polyolefins, wire and cable insulation, adhesives.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 10.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

Sulfur-containing hindered phenol; oxidises to sulfoxide/sulfone during storage
