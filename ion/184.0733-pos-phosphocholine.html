At m/z 184.0733 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of Phosphocholine (C5H14NO4P, monoisotopic 183.0660).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.

Open m/z 184.0733 in the finder →

Where it comes from

Phospholipids of any plasma, serum, tissue or cell sample.

Other sources report the same ion from: Zwitterionic phosphorylcholine HILIC phases (SeQuant ZIC-cHILIC); Also the classic phosphatidylcholine head-group fragment.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.5, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • The m/z 184.0733 phosphocholine head-group fragment is produced by every PC and SM species; it dominates positive-mode MS2 and precursor-184 scans…
  • Intact ligand head group of ZIC-cHILIC released by ester hydrolysis. The 184.0733 cation is also the universal phosphatidylcholine in-source…
