Where it comes from

Slip additive in polyolefin labware and films.

Other sources report the same ion from: Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction); Polypropylene disposable pipette tips (Eppendorf/Fisher).

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 9.0, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Oleamide, erucamide and stearamide, added to plastics as slip agents; notorious for appearing in positive mode from pipette tips and bags. More slip agents and fatty amides →

How to recognize it

  • Saturated partner of erucamide; 2 Da above erucamide
