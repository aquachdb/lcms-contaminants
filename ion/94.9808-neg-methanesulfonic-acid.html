  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Ionic surfactants and detergents.

Where it comes from

Ion-pairing/mobile phase acid.

Other sources report the same ion from: Ion-chromatography eluent / acidic modifier; Mesylate counter-ions of drug salts; MSA additive.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.9, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →
