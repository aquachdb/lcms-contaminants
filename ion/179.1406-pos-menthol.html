Where it comes from

Mint flavor/fragrance in toothpaste, balms and wipes.

Chromatography: elutes late reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.0, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

Poor ESI; usually water-loss ion 139.1481 or sodiated 179.1406
