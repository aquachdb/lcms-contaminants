  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.

Where it comes from

Preservative in reagents, buffers, media and consumer product carryover.

Other sources report the same ion from: Fragrance and degradation product of salicylates; Benzoyl chloride derivatization of amines/phenols, and preservative; Extractables & leachables from container-closure systems, stoppers, syringes and single-use bioprocess….

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient on C18.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.9, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Preservatives from reagents, buffers and consumables, including biocides in water systems. More antimicrobials and preservatives →

How to recognize it

  • Very common negative-mode background; fragment 77.0386
  • ESI- 121.0295
  • Excess benzoyl chloride hydrolyses to benzoic acid, giving a large background peak; also a food/preservative contaminant
