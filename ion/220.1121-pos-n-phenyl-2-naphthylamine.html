Where it comes from

Rubber antioxidant in tubing, seals, gloves, septa.

Other sources report the same ion from: Rubber antioxidant in tubing, seals, gloves; Rubber antioxidant leaching from seals and tubing.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →

How to recognize it

  • Classic rubber antioxidant leachable; strong ESI+
  • Isomeric with N-phenyl-2-naphthylamine; separate chromatographically
  • Detected in the procedural BLANK of the NORMAN collaborative trial (blank intensity 3976 vs sample 2810; RP-LC RT 12.71 min); DTXSID DTXSID2025892
