Where it comes from

Cholesterol-bonded phases (Cosmosil Cholester, Ascentis Cholesterol) after ester or ether cleavage; also ubiquitous biological background.

Chromatography: elutes very late/highly lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.7, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
