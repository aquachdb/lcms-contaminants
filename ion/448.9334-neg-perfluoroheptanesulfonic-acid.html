Where it comes from

LC system PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottle caps and SPE consumables; short-chain PFAS target of EPA Method 533.

Other sources report the same ion from: PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, LC seals, solvents and glassware.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • ESI- only; fragments 79.9574 and 98.9556; sulfonates are the most persistent LC system PFAS background
  • EPA Method 533 analyte (short-chain/ether PFAS, C4-C12). Listed in the EPA method comparison table with CASRN 375-92-8 but no m/z is given in that…
