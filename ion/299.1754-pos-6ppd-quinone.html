Where it comes from

Oxidation product of the tyre antiozonant 6PPD, road runoff, rubber articles.

Other sources report the same ion from: Tire rubber oxidation product in road runoff and ambient dust.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →

How to recognise it

  • Fragments to m/z 215.0815 and 241.1335; well-known environmental transformation product
  • ESI+ 299.1754; ambient/roadway water contaminant
