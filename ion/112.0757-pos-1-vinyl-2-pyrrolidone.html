Where it comes from

Residual monomer from polymeric SPE sorbents (Oasis HLB type) and PVP plasticware.

Chromatography: elutes early.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.4, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

Monomer leached from divinylbenzene/N-vinylpyrrolidone SPE sorbent; the oligomer series has 111.068 spacing
