Where it comes from

Chloramination disinfection byproduct in water, also from amine-containing reagents.

Chromatography: void volume to early.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.6, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds that arrive through the laboratory air or water supply rather than through the sample. More ambient and environmental background →

How to recognize it

ESI+ 75.0553 weak; usually APCI+; regulated trace water contaminant
