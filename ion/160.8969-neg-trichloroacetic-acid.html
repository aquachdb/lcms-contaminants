Where it comes from

Chlorination disinfection byproduct in tap/lab water, also protein-precipitation reagent.

Other sources report the same ion from: Protein precipitation.

Chromatography: void volume to early.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds that arrive through the laboratory air or water supply rather than through the sample. More ambient and environmental background →

How to recognize it

  • ESI- 160.8964 with Cl3 isotope pattern; decarboxylates in source to CCl3- 116.906
  • Distinctive Cl3 isotope pattern (M+2 ~98%); persists after incomplete washing
