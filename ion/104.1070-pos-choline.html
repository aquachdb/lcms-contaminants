Where it comes from

Cell culture media component, ubiquitous biological matrix ion.

Other sources report the same ion from: Skin, sweat and general biological handling background; Zwitterionic phosphorylcholine HILIC phases (ZIC-cHILIC) after phosphate ester loss; Also ubiquitous biological background.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.4, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • Permanent cation 104.1070 with fragment 60.0808; ubiquitous ESI+ background
  • Permanently charged; one of the most intense low-mass ions in any biological extract; MS2 gives 60.081 and 58.065
  • Permanent cation so no protonation is needed; in-source dephosphorylation product of the ZIC-cHILIC ligand. Extremely common biological background…
