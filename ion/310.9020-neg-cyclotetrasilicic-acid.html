Where it comes from

Dissolution of silica-based packings at high pH; cyclic silicate released from the particle surface.

Chromatography: void volume.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Cyclic silicate; cyclic and linear silicates coexist and the cyclic series is 18.0106 below the linear one at the same silicon count
