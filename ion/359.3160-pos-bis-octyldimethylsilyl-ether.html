Where it comes from

Self-condensation of released C8 ligand from octyl reversed-phase columns.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Condensation dimer of octyldimethylsilanol; appears after a column has been stored wet and then dried, and elutes in the organic wash
