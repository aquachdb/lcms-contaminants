Where it comes from

PVC cling film, food packaging, gaskets, tubing.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.8, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →
