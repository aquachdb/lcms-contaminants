Where it comes from

Hydraulic fluids, PVC plasticizer, flame retardant.

Other sources report the same ion from: Tricresyl phosphate flame retardant/plasticizer.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

  • Mixture of ortho/meta/para cresyl isomers
