Where it comes from

PETG plasticware, single-use bioprocess bags and tubing, clear rigid packaging.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it

PETG is PET with part of the ethylene glycol replaced by 1,4-cyclohexanedimethanol (CHDM). The CHDM-terephthalate repeat is C16H18O4 (274.1205)…
