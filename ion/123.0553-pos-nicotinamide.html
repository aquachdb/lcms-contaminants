Where it comes from

Cell culture media vitamin, ubiquitous in biological matrix.

Other sources report the same ion from: Skincare serum and lotion transfer, vitamin supplement.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.4, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognise it

  • ESI+ 123.0553 fragment 80.049 and 96.044
  • Very high abundance in media and in every biological extract; MS2 gives 96.045 and 80.049
