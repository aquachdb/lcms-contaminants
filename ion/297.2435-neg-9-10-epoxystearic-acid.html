Where it comes from

Epoxidised soybean oil (ESBO) plasticizer/stabiliser in PVC gaskets and jar lids.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

ESBO is a mixture; epoxy fatty acid markers are released on transesterification/hydrolysis
