Where it comes from

Residue from Wittig/Mitsunobu synthesis, plastics and reagent-grade solvents; extremely common ESI+ background.

Other sources report the same ion from: Synthesis residue, plastic additive.

Chromatography: elutes late reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.8, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
