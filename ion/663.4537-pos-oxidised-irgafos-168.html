Where it comes from

Extractable/leachable from single-use polymeric films and bioprocess bags (LC-HRMS E&L screening).

Other sources report the same ion from: Oxidation product of Irgafos 168 in aged polyolefin plastics; Extractables & leachables from container-closure systems, stoppers, syringes and single-use bioprocess….

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 15.0, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →

How to recognise it

  • Usually more abundant than the parent phosphite in aged plastic; the classic pipette-tip/tube background pair with m/z 647
  • Also observed as [M+NH4]+ at m/z 680.4804; from the Full MS/ddMS2 inclusion list used for E&L screening of single-use plastic film
  • Oxidation product of Irgafos 168 in the E&L standard mixture; member of a certified E&L reference standard mixture analysed by LC-HRMS with UV and CAD
