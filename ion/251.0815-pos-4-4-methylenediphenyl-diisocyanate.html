Where it comes from

Polyurethane tubing, foams, adhesives.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.4, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it

Highly reactive; usually detected as MDA after hydrolysis or as methanol/amine derivatives
