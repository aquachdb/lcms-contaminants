Where it comes from

Hydrolysis product of BADGE in epoxy can linings.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.1, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it
