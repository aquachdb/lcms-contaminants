At m/z 555.8896 in positive ion mode, this is [6M+O+H2O+3Fe-6H]+ of Acetic acid-Fe-O- complex (C2H4O2, monoisotopic 60.0211).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Miscellaneous background ions, Salt clusters.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

During ESI with metal tips and acetic acid.

Other sources report the same ion from: Solvent; Corrosion of metal ESI emitter with acetic acid; Corrosion of stainless steel ESI emitter/tubing with acetic acid.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.2, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →
