Where it comes from

Anionic detergent used in gel electrophoresis and lysis buffers.

Other sources report the same ion from: SDS-containing lysis buffers and detergent residue.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

  • Sodium-bound cluster of two SDS salt units minus one Na+; very diagnostic of SDS carryover; cluster formula given minus the charge carrier
  • SDS cluster series with 288.13713 spacing, reported in negative mode only; must be removed before LC-MS
