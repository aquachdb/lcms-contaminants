Where it comes from

Bitterant in denatured ethanol.

Other sources report the same ion from: Photoinitiator co-synergist in printing inks, packaging migrant; Bittering/denaturant additive in low-purity ethanol and solvents.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
