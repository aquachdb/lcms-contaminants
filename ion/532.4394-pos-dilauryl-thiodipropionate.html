Where it comes from

Secondary (peroxide-decomposing) antioxidant in polyolefins and packaging.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 11.7, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

Thioether antioxidant; oxidises to sulfoxide (+16) on aging
