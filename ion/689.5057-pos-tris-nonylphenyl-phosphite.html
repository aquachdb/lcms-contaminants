Where it comes from

Stabiliser in PVC, rubber, polyolefins, food-contact plastics.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 19.3, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →

How to recognise it

Branched nonyl isomer envelope; hydrolyses to nonylphenol
