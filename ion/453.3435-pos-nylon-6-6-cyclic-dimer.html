Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it

  • 113.0841 spacing; isomeric with the nylon-6,6 cyclic dimer
  • 226.1681 repeat spacing; isomeric with nylon-6 cyclic tetramer
  • 113.0841 spacing; also seen as [M+Na]+
