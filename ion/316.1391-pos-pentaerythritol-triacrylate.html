Where it comes from

UV-curable inks and coatings.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.1, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it

Technical grade contains tetra-acrylate as well
