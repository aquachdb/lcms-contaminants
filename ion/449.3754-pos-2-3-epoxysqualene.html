Where it comes from

Air-oxidized skin squalene on glassware and vials.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 10.3, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognise it

Ozone/UV oxidation product of skin squalene; +15.9949 from squalene; increases in aged or air-exposed samples
