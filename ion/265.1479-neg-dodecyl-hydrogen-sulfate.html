Where it comes from

SDS-PAGE and protein lysis buffers, lab dish detergent residue, glassware carryover.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hand soap, shampoo, lab detergent and SDS-PAGE reagent residue; SDS from protein sample preparation and detergent residue on glassware; Anionic detergent (SDS-type) from cleaning agents and protein buffers.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.7, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →
