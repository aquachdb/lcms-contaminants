At m/z 122.0812 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of Tris(hydroxymethyl)aminomethane (C4H11NO3, monoisotopic 121.0739).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.

Where it comes from

TRIS buffer used in biochemical sample preparation.

Other sources report the same ion from: Autoxidation of unstabilised THF; Protein/nucleic acid buffers, lysis and storage buffers; Buffer.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -2.9, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Tris, HEPES, MES, MOPS, PIPES and similar Good's buffers carried over from sample preparation. More biological buffers →

How to recognise it

  • Ammoniated THF hydroperoxide
  • Dominant early-eluting background in positive ESI from any Tris-buffered sample; strongly suppresses; remove by SPE or desalting
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 122.0811704
