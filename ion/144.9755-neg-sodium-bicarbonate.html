Where it comes from

Bicarbonate buffer residue; CO2 uptake by alkaline mobile phase.

Chromatography: void volume.

Sodium formate, sodium acetate, sodium trifluoroacetate, cesium and sodium iodide ladders. Used as calibrants on purpose and seen by accident whenever salt reaches the source. More salt clusters →
