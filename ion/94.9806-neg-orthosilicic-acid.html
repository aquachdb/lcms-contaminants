At m/z 94.9806 in negative ion mode, this is [M-H]- of Orthosilicic acid (H4O4Si, monoisotopic 95.9879).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Miscellaneous background ions.

Where it comes from

Dissolution of bare silica and silica-based HILIC/reversed-phase packings, especially above pH 8.

Other sources report the same ion from: Reagents, glassware, biological matrix and ambient contamination.

Chromatography: void volume.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

  • Deprotonated orthosilicic acid from dissolving glassware and fused silica; worse at high pH
  • The primary product of silica dissolution. Poor ESI responder and low mass, so it is usually seen only as a broad void-volume signal in negative…
