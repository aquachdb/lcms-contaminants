Where it comes from

Fragrance in personal care, tonka/vanilla flavor, airborne.

Chromatography: elutes mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.4, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →
