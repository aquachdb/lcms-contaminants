Where it comes from

Diisononyl phthalate in PVC labware and tubing.

Other sources report the same ion from: Flexible PVC, tubing, gaskets, toys, packaging; PVC plastics, tubing; Plastic tubing, vials, gloves, caps.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 9.6, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Technical mixture of branched C9 isomers, gives a broad hump not a sharp peak; fragment m/z 149.0233 and 293.1747
  • Mixed-ester phthalate; isomeric with diisononyl phthalate; n-Octyl n-decyl phthalate
  • Detected in the procedural BLANK of the NORMAN collaborative trial (blank intensity 5.99852e+06 vs sample 8.94673e+06; RP-LC RT 19.149 min); DTXSID…
