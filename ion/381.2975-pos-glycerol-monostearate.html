Where it comes from

Internal antistatic and antifog additive in polypropylene tubes, plates and films.

Other sources report the same ion from: Detergent; Reduced non-ionic detergent in protein sample prep; Reduced detergent in lysis buffers.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionize extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognize it

  • Common polyolefin labware leachate; [M+NH4]+ 376.3421 and [M+H-H2O]+ 341.3050 also seen; mixture of 1- and 2-monoglyceride isomers
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 381.2975314
  • Oligomer n=3, C15H30O hydrophobe; 44.0262 repeat spacing; 101R Detergents
