Where it comes from

Acetic acid / ammonium acetate mobile-phase additive.

Other sources report the same ion from: 0.1 percent acetic acid modifier; Cornstarch glove powder, paper and general handling residue; Cell culture media component, tissue/plasma matrix.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.2, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →

How to recognize it

  • [3AcOH-H]- trimer anion
  • From powdered gloves and starch; ESI- as [M+Cl]- 215.0328 or [M-H]- 179.0561; poorly retained on C18
  • Isobaric with glucose; separated only chromatographically
