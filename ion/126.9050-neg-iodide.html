Where it comes from

Iodide salts/reagents and catalyst residues.

Other sources report the same ion from: ESI calibration solution; High-mass ESI calibration standard; Reagents, glassware, biological matrix and ambient contamination.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.9, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it

  • [(NaI)n+I]- calibrant series; repeat mass 149.8942 Da; useful n=0-10
  • [(CsI)n+I]- series; repeat mass 259.8099 Da; useful n=0-10
  • Iodide; monoisotopic; from iodide reagents, contrast media and thyroid-related matrix
