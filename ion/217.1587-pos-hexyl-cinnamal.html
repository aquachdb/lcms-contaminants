Where it comes from

Jasmine-type fragrance allergen in soaps and detergents.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.8, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

ESI+ 217.1587 often with water loss; declared EU fragrance allergen
