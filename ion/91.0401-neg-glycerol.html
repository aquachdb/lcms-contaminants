Where it comes from

Hand lotion, soap and cosmetic humectant transfer.

Other sources report the same ion from: Enzyme/protein storage buffers; Hydrolysis of diol and cross-linked diol HILIC phases; Also ubiquitous laboratory background.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.8, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognise it

  • Poor retention on C18; ESI+ sodiated 115.0366; often suppresses early-eluting analytes
  • Weak negative-mode ion
  • Terminal hydrolysis product of a diol phase but far too common as a general background (enzyme storage, plasticiser, glassware) to be diagnostic on…
