Where it comes from

Benzoyl chloride derivatization quenched in methanol.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.1, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognize it

Formed when benzoylation reactions are quenched or diluted with methanol
