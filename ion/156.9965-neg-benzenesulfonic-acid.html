Where it comes from

Benzenesulfonic-acid SCX and mixed-mode cation-exchange phases (Luna SCX, Partisil SCX type).

Other sources report the same ion from: Sulfonated aromatic from dyes, detergents and hydrotropes.

Chromatography: void volume; retained in HILIC segment.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →
