Where it comes from

Skin and sebum transfer from fingers, hair, ambient dust.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic, often at column wash.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 11.6, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it
