Where it comes from

Chaotrope for protein denaturation, 6-8 M lysis buffers.

Other sources report the same ion from: Sweat and fingerprint transfer, hand cream humectant; Plasma/serum and urine matrix (in addition to its use as a chaotrope).

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.4, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Derivatisation reagents, chaotropes, reducing and alkylating agents, and extraction additives. More sample-preparation reagents →

How to recognise it

  • Poorly retained; weak ESI+ 61.0396; better on HILIC
  • Ubiquitous in proteomics; decomposes to ammonium cyanate causing +43.0058 carbamylation of peptide N-termini and Lys
  • Endogenous urea in plasma/urine is a major low-mass background ion independent of any denaturing buffer
