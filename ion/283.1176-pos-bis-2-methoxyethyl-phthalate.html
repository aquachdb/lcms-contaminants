Where it comes from

Cellulose ester plastics, coatings, older PVC formulations.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.4, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

More polar than alkyl phthalates; fragment m/z 149.0233
