Where it comes from

Other sources report the same ion from: Antistatic quat leached from disposable polypropylene labware and pipette tips; Disposable plastic labware (tubes, tips) reported leachable.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.2, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it

  • Reported with oleamide as a bioactive leachable from disposable plasticware; chain length can be C12 to C18 in technical antistat blends so check the…
  • Very common lotion carryover; ESI+ ammonium/sodium adducts; APCI+ acylium 211.2
  • Permanently charged quaternary ammonium leached from disposable plasticware; reported as a bioactive plastic leachable alongside oleamide
