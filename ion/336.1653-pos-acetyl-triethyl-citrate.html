Where it comes from

Pharmaceutical film coatings, food-contact plastics.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.6, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →
