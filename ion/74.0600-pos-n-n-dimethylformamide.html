At m/z 74.0600 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of N,N-Dimethylformamide (C3H7NO, monoisotopic 73.0528).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.

Where it comes from

Synthesis/stock solvent carried into the sample.

Other sources report the same ion from: Mixed ACN-methanol mobile phase; Solvent; Synthesis solvent carryover.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.0, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognize it

  • ACN+CH3OH+H mixed proton-bound cluster; isobaric with protonated DMF
  • Protonated DMF; isobaric with ACN+MeOH+H cluster
