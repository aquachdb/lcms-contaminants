Where it comes from

Skin and sweat transfer.

Other sources report the same ion from: Shampoo/detergent foam stabilizer, cosmetics, hand soap residue on glassware.

Chromatography: elutes mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.2, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • ESI+ 300.2897 with water losses 282.28 and 264.2686
