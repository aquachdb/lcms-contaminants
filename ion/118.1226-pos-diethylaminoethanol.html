Where it comes from

Boiler/steam-line neutralizing amine.

Other sources report the same ion from: Diethylaminoethyl (DEAE) weak anion-exchange and mixed-mode phases.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds that arrive through the laboratory air or water supply rather than through the sample. More ambient and environmental background →

How to recognize it

  • Steam amine; ESI+ 118.1226
  • Head group of a DEAE weak anion exchanger; strongly basic, protonates efficiently and can dominate a low-mass positive background on WAX columns
