Where it comes from

Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Other sources report the same ion from: Skin/sebum transfer, dietary lipid carryover; Human sebum-specific fatty acid; Plasma, FBS in media.

Chromatography: standardized RT 5.25 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.8, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • 279.2330 in ESI-; ubiquitous blank ion
  • Sebum-specific 18:2 isomer, same mass as linoleic acid; resolve by RT
  • Abundant plasma/serum free fatty acid
