Where it comes from

Carbonyl protection (methoximation) before silylation.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.7, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Derivatisation reagents, chaotropes, reducing and alkylating agents, and extraction additives. More sample-preparation reagents →

How to recognise it

Primarily a GC-MS derivatization reagent; in LC-MS it is seen mainly as methoxime adducts (+29.0265, CH3N minus H) on carbonyl metabolites
