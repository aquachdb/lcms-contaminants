Where it comes from

Zwitterionic sulfoalkylbetaine HILIC phases (ZIC-HILIC, ZIC-pHILIC, Atlantis BEH Z-HILIC) released by siloxane hydrolysis.

Chromatography: retained in HILIC segment.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Whole ligand plus its silicon released by hydrolysis of the Si-O-Si anchor; the silanetriol form. Silicon gives a diagnostic M+1 of about 5 percent…
