Where it comes from

Fragrance and preservative in cosmetics and injectables.

Chromatography: elutes early/mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.1, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →
