Where it comes from

Poly(2-sulfoethyl aspartamide) SCX/HILIC columns (PolyLC PolySULFOETHYL A) after amide hydrolysis.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasma, tissue, cell extracts, media supplement.

Chromatography: retained in HILIC segment; void volume on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -4.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

  • Very polar, elutes at the void on reversed phase; abundant in cells and tissue
