Where it comes from

Plasma, tissue, cell extracts, media supplement.

Other sources report the same ion from: Poly(2-sulfoethyl aspartamide) SCX/HILIC columns (PolyLC PolySULFOETHYL A) after amide hydrolysis.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -4.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • Very polar, elutes at the void on reversed phase; abundant in cells and tissue
