Where it comes from

Polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes, pipette tips, low-bind plasticware.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hindered amine light stabiliser in coatings and plastics.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.4, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • N-methylated analogue of Tinuvin 770; component of Tinuvin 292
  • Widely reported leachable from polypropylene labware; very strong positive ESI response, detectable at trace level from a single tube
