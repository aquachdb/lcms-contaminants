Where it comes from

Quat disinfectant and sanitizer residue, preservative in reagents.

Other sources report the same ion from: Quaternary ammonium sanitizer blends used on benches and glassware; Disinfectant wipes and bench cleaner residue; Cationic surfactant and biocide in disinfectant wipes, hair conditioner, preservative in reagents.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

  • Second dominant BAC homolog; 28.0313 above BAC-C12
  • Isobaric with BAC-C14 so retention time or MS2 is needed to distinguish; both occur in dual-quat sanitizers
  • Permanent cation 332.3312; homolog spacing 28.0313; fragment 91.0542
