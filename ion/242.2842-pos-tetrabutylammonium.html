At m/z 242.2842 in positive ion mode, this is [M]+ of Tetrabutylammonium (C16H36N, monoisotopic 242.2848).

Check it on PubChem: CID 16028 ↗

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Biological buffers, Ionic surfactants and detergents, Mobile-phase additives.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

TBA hydrogensulfate/phosphate/acetate ion-pairing systems and phase-transfer catalysis.

Other sources report the same ion from: Buffer; Solvent or base carryover; Ion-pairing reagent / quaternary ammonium residue.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

TFA, HFBA, triethylamine, hexylamine and related reagents, which persist in an LC system for a very long time after the method that used them. More ion-pairing reagents →

How to recognize it

  • Permanent quaternary cation; one of the worst persistent carryover contaminants in LC-MS - survives months of washing and suppresses positive-mode…
