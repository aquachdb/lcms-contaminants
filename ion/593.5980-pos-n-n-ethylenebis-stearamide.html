Where it comes from

Mold release and lubricant in molded plastic labware, tube caps and filter housings.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 15.7, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Oleamide, erucamide and stearamide, added to plastics as slip agents; notorious for appearing in positive mode from pipette tips and bags. More slip agents and fatty amides →

How to recognize it

High-mass wax lubricant leachate; often needs 100 percent organic to elute; also [M+Na]+ 615.5798
