Where it comes from

Extractable/leachable from single-use polymeric films and bioprocess bags (LC-HRMS E&L screening).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.8, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognize it

Cross-linking co-agent extractable from polymeric film; from the Full MS/ddMS2 inclusion list used for E&L screening of single-use plastic film
