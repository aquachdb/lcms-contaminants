Where it comes from

Bicarbonate buffer residue.

Chromatography: void volume.

Sodium formate, sodium acetate, sodium trifluoroacetate, caesium and sodium iodide ladders. Used as calibrants on purpose and seen by accident whenever salt reaches the source. More salt clusters →

How to recognise it

[(NaHCO3)+Na]+; readily loses CO2 in-source to give the NaOH-Na cluster at 62.9817
