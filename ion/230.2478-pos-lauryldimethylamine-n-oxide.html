Where it comes from

Membrane protein solubilization, mild detergent stocks.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognise it

Amine oxide detergent; very efficient positive-mode ionizer so trace amounts dominate spectra; loss of O and water fragments
