Where it comes from

Phenolic resin residue, polycarbonate chain terminator, adhesives.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.3, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognize it

Note the nominal coincidence with the m/z 149 phthalate fragment in positive mode - different polarity and formula
