Where it comes from

Cellulose esters, coatings, older plastics.

Chromatography: elutes late on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.5, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

Do not confuse the abbreviation with diphenyl phosphate
