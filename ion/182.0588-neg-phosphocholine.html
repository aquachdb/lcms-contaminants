Where it comes from

Zwitterionic phosphorylcholine HILIC phases (SeQuant ZIC-cHILIC); also the classic phosphatidylcholine head-group fragment.

Chromatography: retained in HILIC segment.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.5, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Intact ligand head group of ZIC-cHILIC released by ester hydrolysis. The 184.0733 cation is also the universal phosphatidylcholine in-source…
