At m/z 167.0339 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of Phthalic acid (C8H6O4, monoisotopic 166.0266).

Check it on PubChem: CID 1017 ↗

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Miscellaneous background ions.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

Hydrolysis of phthalate esters, in-source fragment m/z 167.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hydrolysis product of phthalate esters (e.g. dioctyl phthalate); Hydrolysis product of phthalate ester plasticizers.

Chromatography: void volume to early eluting.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.7, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognizable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

  • Protonated phthalic acid m/z 167.0339 dehydrates in source to m/z 149.0233
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 167.0338864
  • Instrument: API366
