Where it comes from

Sunscreen and daily moisturizer transfer from hands.

Other sources report the same ion from: UV filter from sunscreen on skin and gloves.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.1, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

  • Very common ESI+ background from sunscreen; 362.2115 with fragments 250.0863 and 232.0757
  • Detected in the procedural BLANK of the NORMAN collaborative trial (blank intensity 16700 vs sample 12516; RP-LC RT 14.26 min); DTXSID DTXSID9025299
  • MassBank EU record MSBNK-ACES_SU-AS000126 (LC-APCI-QFT); reported RT 20.252200
