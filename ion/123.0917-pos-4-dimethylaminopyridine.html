Check it on PubChem: CID 14284 ↗

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Other polymer oligomers, Solvent and mobile-phase impurities.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

Acylation catalyst in organic synthesis.

Other sources report the same ion from: Polyurethane hydrolysis, TDI residue in PU foams and adhesives; Solvent; Synthesis catalyst carryover.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
