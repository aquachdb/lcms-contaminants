Where it comes from

Plastic tubing and caps, solvent stabilizer (THF/ether), food packaging, gloves.

Other sources report the same ion from: Degradation product of nonylphenol ethoxylate surfactants, plastic and rubber additives; Hydrolysis or degradation product of NP-40 and Igepal detergents, plastics and environmental carryover; Antioxidant in plastic labware, filter membranes, THF and ether stabilizer.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

  • Best detected in ESI-/APCI-; extremely common laboratory background from solvents and plastics
  • Technical material is a mixture of branched isomers giving a cluster of partially resolved peaks; isomeric with BHT
  • Branched isomer mixture giving a broad or multi-peak profile; parent phenol of the NP-40 ethoxylate series (ethoxamers covered elsewhere)
