Where it comes from

Cell culture media, glutamine degradation product, plasma matrix.

Other sources report the same ion from: Fingerprint and sweat amino acid transfer.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -3.7, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
