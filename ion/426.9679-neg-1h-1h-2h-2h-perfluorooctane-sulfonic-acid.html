Where it comes from

LC system PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottle caps and SPE consumables; short-chain PFAS target of EPA Method 533.

Other sources report the same ion from: PFAS background from fluoropolymer components, aqueous film-forming foam and coatings.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.9, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • ESI- 426.9680 with fragments 406.96 (HF loss), 80.9646 and 79.9574
  • EPA Method 533 analyte (short-chain/ether PFAS, C4-C12). Listed in the EPA method comparison table with CASRN 27619-97-2 but no m/z is given in that…
