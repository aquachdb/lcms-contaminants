Where it comes from

Hydrolysis of ethyl acetate; also an additive.

Other sources report the same ion from: 0.1 percent acetic acid mobile-phase modifier; 2-20 mM ammonium acetate buffer; Sodium contamination in acetate-buffered mobile phase.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.2, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Impurities, stabilizers and degradation products carried in by the solvents themselves, plus their cluster ions. More solvent and mobile-phase impurities →

How to recognize it

  • Acetate from hydrolyzed ethyl acetate as well as from acetate buffers
  • Acetate anion; dominant ESI- background in acetate mobile phases; forms [M+CH3COO]- adducts
  • Negative-mode cluster [(NH4OAc)n+OAc]-; repeat mass 77.0477 Da; useful n=0-5
