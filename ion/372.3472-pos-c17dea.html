Where it comes from

Cocamide DEA foam booster in soaps and cleaners.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.8, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognise it

NORMAN-SLE S7 EAWAGSURF suspect 'C17DEA'; nonionic surfactant; ESI+ response is usually dominated by [M+NH4]+ and [M+Na]+ rather than [M+H]+
