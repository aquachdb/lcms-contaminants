Where it comes from

Permeabilization buffers, organelle fractionation.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -3.9, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionize extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →
