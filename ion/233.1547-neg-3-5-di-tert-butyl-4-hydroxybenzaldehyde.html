Where it comes from

Oxidation product of BHT and of hindered-phenol antioxidants.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.4, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

Common BHT degradation marker in aged plastics
