Where it comes from

Hindered amine light stabilizer in coatings and plastics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes, pipette tips, low-bind plasticware.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.4, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

  • N-methylated analog of Tinuvin 770; component of Tinuvin 292
  • Widely reported leachable from polypropylene labware; very strong positive ESI response, detectable at trace level from a single tube
