Where it comes from

Epoxy novolac can coatings, adhesives.

Other sources report the same ion from: Vinyl flooring, sealants, PVC tubing, conveyor belting.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.2, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • Ammonium adduct with ammonium acetate
  • Isomer mixture; isobaric with benzyl butyl phthalate ammonium adduct
