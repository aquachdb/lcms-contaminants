Where it comes from

Butyl (C4) reversed-phase columns used for proteins and peptides (Zorbax 300SB-C3/C4, BEH C4, Vydac C4).

Chromatography: elutes early.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Released C4 ligand; short-chain phases are the least hydrolytically stable of the alkyl series so C4 columns bleed ligand faster than C18 at the same…
