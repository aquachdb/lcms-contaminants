Where it comes from

Residual amine from cocamidopropyl betaine hand soaps.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.3, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →
