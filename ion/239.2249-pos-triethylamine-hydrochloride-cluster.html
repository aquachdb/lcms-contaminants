Where it comes from

Ion-pairing buffer for oligonucleotide LC-MS.

Other sources report the same ion from: Buffer salt; TEA buffer with chloride; Ion-pairing/mobile phase additive salt.

Chromatography: void volume.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →

How to recognise it

  • Triethylamine-hydrochloride, buffer; proton-bound dimer of the amine hydrochloride, [(M.HCl)2-Cl]+
  • Bis-triethylamine hydrochloride cluster; 35Cl form, 37Cl partner at m/z 241.22190
  • Solved as [2(C6H15N)+HCl+H]+; 35Cl form
