Other sources report the same ion from: Reagents, glassware, biological matrix and ambient contamination; Saliva/handling transfer and water background.

How to recognize it

  • Thiocyanate; saliva/biological matrix and smoker samples; a very common ESI- background ion
  • ESI- 57.9757; common in saliva-contaminated and smoker samples
  • Thiocyanate anion; strong negative-mode ion and severe suppressor, low m/z
