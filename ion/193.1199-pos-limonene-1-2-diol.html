Where it comes from

Limonene autoxidation product from citrus cleaners.

Chromatography: elutes mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.5, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognise it

Secondary oxidation product; usually as [M+H-H2O]+ 153.127 or sodiated
