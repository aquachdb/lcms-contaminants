At m/z 152.0165 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of 1,2-Benzisothiazolin-3-one (C7H5NOS, monoisotopic 151.0092).

Check it on PubChem: CID 17520 ↗

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Tubing and seal materials, Labware leachables.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.
  • No published contaminant list cited here reports this entry. It comes from this project's own domain-knowledge half — cross-checked, but not traceable to a citable table. Treat it as a lead to confirm rather than as an attested observation.

Where it comes from

Preservative in aqueous reagents, buffers and cleaning products.

Other sources report the same ion from: Rubber degradation product from septa, stoppers, tubing; Rubber degradation product in water and glove leachate.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient on C18.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Preservatives from reagents, buffers and consumables, including biocides in water systems. More antimicrobials and preservatives →

How to recognize it

  • Oxidative degradation product of MBT
  • Common industrial in-can preservative
  • ESI+ 152.0165, ESI- 150.0019
