Where it comes from

Slip agent in polyethylene films and plastic tubes.

Other sources report the same ion from: Slip agent from polypropylene labware and syringes; Slip agent; Slip agent in polyethylene films.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.6, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Oleamide, erucamide and stearamide, added to plastics as slip agents; notorious for appearing in positive mode from pipette tips and bags. More slip agents and fatty amides →

How to recognize it

  • Sodiated oleamide; often accompanies the 282.2791 ion
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 304.2610854
