At m/z 149.0233 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of Phthalic anhydride (in-source fragment of phthalate esters) (C8H4O3, monoisotopic 148.0160).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Miscellaneous background ions, Non-phthalate plasticizers.
Open m/z 149.0233 in the finder →

Where it comes from

In-source fragmentation of dialkyl phthalate plasticisers from tubing, vials and gloves.

Other sources report the same ion from: In-source and CID fragment of nearly all ortho-phthalate diesters; In-source fragmentation of any ortho-phthalate ester leached from plastics; Plasticiser.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • THE universal phthalate marker ion C8H5O3+ at m/z 149.0233; also present as an in-source background ion; further loses CO to m/z 121.0284
  • The classic m/z 149 background ion; a protonated phthalic anhydride formed from DBP/DEHP/BBP; present in essentially every positive-mode spectrum…
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 149.0233214
