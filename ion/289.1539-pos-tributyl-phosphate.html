Where it comes from

Flame retardant/plasticizer in plastics, foams and coatings.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastics, hydraulic fluids, antifoam, solvent extraction residue; Plastic tubing and filter extractable; Plasticizer/antifoam in plastics, tubing and filters.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.9, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
