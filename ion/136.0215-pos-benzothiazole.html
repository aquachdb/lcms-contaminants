Where it comes from

Rubber septa, tubing, gaskets, O-rings, vulcanisation residue.

Other sources report the same ion from: Rubber septa, stoppers, gloves and tubing vulcanization residue; Rubber and tire degradation product, glove and tubing leachate, ambient air.

Chromatography: elutes early to mid.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.0, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →

How to recognize it

  • Very common rubber/septum background; sharp early peak
  • Classic rubber and glove leachate; also from vial septa
  • ESI+ 136.0215; common rubber-related ambient background
