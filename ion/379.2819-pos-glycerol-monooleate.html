Where it comes from

Antistatic and slip additive in plastic labware, lipidic cubic phase reagent.

Other sources report the same ion from: Sebum monoacylglycerol, cosmetic emulsifier.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.5, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionize extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognize it

  • 2 Da below GMS; also from LCP crystallization work
  • Common lotion/sebum ion; water loss to 339.29 in ESI+
