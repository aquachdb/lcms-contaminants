Where it comes from

Slip additive and plastic leachate.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Oleamide, erucamide and stearamide, added to plastics as slip agents; notorious for appearing in positive mode from pipette tips and bags. More slip agents and fatty amides →

How to recognize it

2 Da below oleamide; minor companion in technical slip agent blends
