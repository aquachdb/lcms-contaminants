Where it comes from

Lyoprotectant/cryoprotectant, density gradients, formulation buffers.

Other sources report the same ion from: Cornstarch glove powder and starch hydrolysis residue.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -3.7, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Derivatization reagents, chaotropes, reducing and alkylating agents, and extraction additives. More sample-preparation reagents →

How to recognize it

  • Starch-derived disaccharide from glove powder; ESI- 341.1089 or sodiated 365.1054
  • Sodiated adduct dominates; trehalose has the same formula so the two are indistinguishable by mass alone
