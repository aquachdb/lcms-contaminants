Where it comes from

UV stabilizer in plastics, coatings, packaging.

Other sources report the same ion from: UV stabilizer in plastics and coatings.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.3, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →

How to recognize it

  • Also sold as Cyasorb UV-5411
  • Isomeric with Tinuvin 329; separate chromatographically
