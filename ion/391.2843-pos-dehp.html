At m/z 391.2843 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of DEHP (C24H38O4, monoisotopic 390.2770).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Non-phthalate plasticizers.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Where it comes from

Plastic tubing, vials, gloves, caps.

Other sources report the same ion from: PVC tubing and blood bags, plastic vials, gloves, syringe filters; PVC articles, tubing, cable insulation; Non-phthalate PVC plasticizer in tubing, medical devices, packaging.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • The most common plasticizer background ion in LC-MS; CID gives m/z 279.1591 (MEHP) and 149.0233
  • Isomeric with DEHP; separated chromatographically (DnOP elutes later)
  • Protonated form; terephthalate isomers give 149 only weakly, a useful discriminator from DEHP
