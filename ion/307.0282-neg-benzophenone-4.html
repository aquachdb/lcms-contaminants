Where it comes from

Water-soluble sunscreen UV filter, hand transfer.

Chromatography: elutes early reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.2, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →
