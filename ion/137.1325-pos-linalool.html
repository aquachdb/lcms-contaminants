Where it comes from

Fragrance in soaps, cleaners and perfumes; airborne transfer.

Other sources report the same ion from: Citrus fragrance in cleaners and hand soaps, airborne solvent; Rose fragrance in soaps and cosmetics.

Chromatography: elutes mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.7, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognise it

  • Tertiary allylic alcohol; almost always seen as water-loss ion 137.1325 in ESI+/APCI+
  • Hydrocarbon terpene, essentially no ESI response; detected via oxidation products or APCI 137.13
  • Primary allylic alcohol; water-loss ion 137.1325 dominates
