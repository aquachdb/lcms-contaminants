At m/z 171.0053 in positive ion mode, this is [M+Na]+ of Phthalate ester (Phthalic anhydride Na) (C8H4O3, monoisotopic 148.0160).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Non-phthalate plasticizers, Miscellaneous background ions.

Where it comes from

Plastic tubing, vials, caps, gloves, solvent bottles.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasticiser; In-source fragment of phthalate esters; Degradation/fragment of phthalate ester plasticisers.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.3, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →
