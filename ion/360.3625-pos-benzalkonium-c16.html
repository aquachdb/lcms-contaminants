Where it comes from

Cationic surfactant and biocide in disinfectant wipes, hair conditioner, preservative in reagents; polyquaternium raw material.

Other sources report the same ion from: Quat disinfectant and sanitizer residue; Disinfectant wipes and bench cleaner residue.

Chromatography: elutes late; notorious for carryover and column memory.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 9.5, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

  • Third BAC homolog; usually least abundant of C12/C14/C16
  • Permanent cation 360.3625; part of BAC homolog comb
  • Permanent quaternary cation observed directly as [M]+ with no adduct. Homologs differ by CH2 (14.0157). Extremely strong ESI response -…
