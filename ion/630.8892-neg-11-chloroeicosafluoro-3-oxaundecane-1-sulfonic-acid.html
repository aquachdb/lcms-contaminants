Where it comes from

LC system PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottle caps and SPE consumables; short-chain PFAS target of EPA Method 533.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.7, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
