Where it comes from

Ammonium salts, ammonium hydroxide, ambient ammonia in the lab.

Other sources report the same ion from: 2-10 mM ammonium formate buffer; 2-20 mM ammonium acetate buffer; Additive that boosts negative-mode ionization (lipidomics).

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.7, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →

How to recognize it

  • Ammonium ion; below most scan ranges but drives [M+NH4]+ adduction throughout the run
  • Positive-mode cluster [(NH4HCO2)n+NH4]+; repeat mass 63.0320 Da; useful n=0-6; intensity scales with buffer concentration and source temperature
  • Positive-mode cluster [(NH4OAc)n+NH4]+; repeat mass 77.0477 Da; useful n=0-5
