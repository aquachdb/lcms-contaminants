Where it comes from

Extractables & leachables from container-closure systems, stoppers, syringes and single-use bioprocess plastics.

Other sources report the same ion from: UV absorber in plastics, coatings and films.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →

How to recognise it

  • Smallest of the benzotriazole UV absorbers; strong ESI+ response
  • UV stabiliser leachable from plastic containers; member of a certified E&L reference standard mixture analysed by LC-HRMS with UV and CAD
