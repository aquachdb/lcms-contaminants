Where it comes from

Artificial sweetener; wastewater and tap-water tracer, coffee/food handling.

Chromatography: elutes early reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.5, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds that arrive through the laboratory air or water supply rather than through the sample. More ambient and environmental background →

How to recognize it

Poor protonation; ESI- as chloride adduct 431.0 dominant and [M-H]- 395.0; Cl3 isotope pattern; classic anthropogenic water tracer
