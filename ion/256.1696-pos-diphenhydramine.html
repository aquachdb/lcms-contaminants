Where it comes from

Common drug/handling background from fingers, dust, shared lab surfaces and tap water.

Chromatography: elutes mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.3, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it

Very common ESI+ background from antihistamine handling; fragment 167.086 and 152.062
