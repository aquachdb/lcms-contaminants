Where it comes from

Corrosion inhibitor in dishwasher detergent, coolants and cooling water; ubiquitous in water blanks.

Chromatography: elutes early/mid reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.0, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
