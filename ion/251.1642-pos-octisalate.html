Where it comes from

Sunscreen transfer from hands.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.7, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

ESI+ 251.1642 with fragment 139.0390; often co-detected with homosalate
