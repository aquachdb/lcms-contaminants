Where it comes from

Precursor and impurity in quat and amine oxide detergents, plastic antistat residue.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

Tertiary amine precursor of LDAO and DTAB; strong ESI+ responder
