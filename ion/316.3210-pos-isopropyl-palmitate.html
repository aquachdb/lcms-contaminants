Where it comes from

Hand cream and cosmetic emollient transfer.

Other sources report the same ion from: Antistatic quat leached from disposable polypropylene labware.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.2, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →

How to recognize it

  • Homolog of the plasticware antistat DiHEMDA
  • Lotion emollient; ammonium/sodium adducts in ESI+
