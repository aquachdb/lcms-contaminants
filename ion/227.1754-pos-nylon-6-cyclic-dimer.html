Where it comes from

Nylon syringe filters, membranes and frits.

Other sources report the same ion from: Nylon-6 filters, frits, membranes, tubing; Nylon-6,6 filters, frits, membranes, tubing.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient on C18.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • Second member of the nylon-6 cyclic oligomer comb; 113.0841 spacing
  • Isomeric with the nylon-6 cyclic dimer - distinguish by source polymer and retention
  • 113.0841 spacing between nylon-6 cyclic oligomers; appears after filtering samples through nylon
