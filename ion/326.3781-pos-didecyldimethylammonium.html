Where it comes from

Quaternary ammonium hard-surface sanitizer, disinfectant wipes on benches.

Other sources report the same ion from: Cationic surfactant and biocide in disinfectant wipes, hair conditioner, preservative in reagents; Polyquaternium raw material.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 9.3, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

  • Main quat in modern surface disinfectants; very persistent positive-mode background
