Where it comes from

Shampoo/detergent foam stabiliser, cosmetics, hand soap residue on glassware.

Other sources report the same ion from: Soap and cleaner residue.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late gradient, later with longer acyl chain.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.0, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognise it

  • Monoethanolamide analogue; fragment 62.0600
  • Fatty acid alkanolamide homolog series; members differ by CH2 (14.0157) and by 2H (2.0157) for unsaturation. Commercial cocamide MEA is a mixture…
