Where it comes from

Flame retardant/plasticizer in floor polish and plastics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Floor polish, plastics, rubber, PVC flooring, indoor dust; Serum separator tube gel and stoppers, plastic tubing, filters and lab plasticware; Rubber stoppers of blood collection tubes and plastic caps.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.8, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

  • Common indoor and laboratory background; fragments to m/z 299.1618 and 199.0730
  • Classic blood-collection-tube and plasticware leachate; strong ESI+ responder at trace level; fragments 199.0730 and 299.1618
  • Classic blood-collection-tube leachable; strong positive-mode ion, displaces analytes from plasma protein binding and suppresses ESI
