Where it comes from

Flame retardant/plasticizer in plastics, foams and coatings.

Other sources report the same ion from: Polyurethane foam flame retardant, insulation, indoor dust.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.6, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
