How to recognise it

  • Isobaric-looking but not isomeric with protonated BHT; distinguishes oxidised from fresh BHT
  • BHT oxidation product; better seen in APCI than ESI
  • BHT oxidation product; near-isobaric with protonated BHT (221.1900 vs 221.1536) - needs >20k resolution to separate
