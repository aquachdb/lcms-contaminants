Where it comes from

Residual monomer in polyDADMAC (polyquaternium-6) flocculants and hair conditioners, water treatment polymer carryover.

Chromatography: void volume, very poorly retained on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.6, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →
