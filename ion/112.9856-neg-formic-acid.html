At m/z 112.9856 in negative ion mode, this is [2M+Na-2H]- of Formic acid (CH2O2, monoisotopic 46.0055).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Salt clusters.

Where it comes from

Mobile phase modifier.

Other sources report the same ion from: ESI calibration solution; Sodium plus formate mobile phase; Mobile phase additive.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.2, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Formic and acetic acid, ammonium salts, TFA and their adducts and clusters -- deliberately added, and a large fraction of any blank. More mobile-phase additives →
