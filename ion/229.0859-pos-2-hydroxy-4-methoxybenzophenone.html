Where it comes from

UV absorber in plastics and coatings, also a sunscreen agent.

Other sources report the same ion from: Sunscreen, lip balm and cosmetic transfer; Fragrance fixative and UV filter in cosmetics; UV filter/photoinitiator from sunscreen and packaging inks.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.6, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognize it

  • Dual use as polymer UV absorber and personal care agent; fragments to m/z 151.0390 and 105.0335
  • ESI+ 229.0859 fragments 151.039 and 105.033; also ESI- 227.0714; same formula as benzyl salicylate
  • Same formula as oxybenzone; ESI+ 229.0859 with fragment 91.0542 tropylium (distinguishes from BP-3)
