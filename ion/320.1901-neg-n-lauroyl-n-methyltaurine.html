Where it comes from

Mild anionic surfactant in cleaners and personal care carryover.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.8, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

Taurine fragments 79.9574 and 124.0074 like bile salt conjugates
