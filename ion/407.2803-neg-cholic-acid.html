Where it comes from

Membrane protein solubilization buffers, bile salt detergent stocks, biological matrix.

Other sources report the same ion from: Membrane protein solubilization.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.6, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

  • Dominant negative-mode ion; water losses at 389.27 and 371.26; also endogenous in bile and serum
  • Same ion as endogenous cholic acid; distinguish by concentration and blanks
