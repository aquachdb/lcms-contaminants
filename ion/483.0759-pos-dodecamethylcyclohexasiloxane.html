Where it comes from

Ambient laboratory air, silicone septa and seals, personal care products, GC and LC column bleed.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient; also present in blank and no-injection runs.

Cyclic (D4-D7) and linear dimethylsiloxanes spaced 74.0188 apart, from vial septa, tubing, seals, column bleed and ambient laboratory air. Their silicon isotope envelope (M+2 about 10-25%) is the giveaway. More siloxanes and silicones →

How to recognize it

Dimethylsiloxane repeat spacing 74.0188 between consecutive oligomers; airborne and septum-derived siloxane background; reduce with clean air supply…
