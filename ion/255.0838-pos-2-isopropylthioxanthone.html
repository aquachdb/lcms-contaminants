Where it comes from

UV printing ink photoinitiator, notorious food packaging migrant.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.1, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognize it

Famous carton/beverage packaging migrant; fragments to m/z 213.0369 and 226.0447
