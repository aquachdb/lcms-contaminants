Where it comes from

UV stabilizer in plastics, films, food packaging.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →
