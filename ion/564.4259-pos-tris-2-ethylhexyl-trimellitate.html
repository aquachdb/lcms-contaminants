Where it comes from

Medical-grade PVC tubing, blood bags, wire insulation.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 10.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

Low-migration DEHP replacement in medical PVC; CID gives m/z 305.1747 and 193.0495
