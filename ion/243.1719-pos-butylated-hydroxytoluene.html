Where it comes from

Antioxidant in solvents and plastics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Stabilizer in THF/ethers; Plastic leachable; Antioxidant.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.3, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilizers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilizers →
