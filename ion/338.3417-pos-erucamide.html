Where it comes from

Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Other sources report the same ion from: Slip agent leached from polypropylene tubes, tips, plates, films and blood collection devices; Slip agent from plastic bags and packaging handled by hand; Anti-static agent.

Chromatography: standardized RT 7.85 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.8, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Oleamide, erucamide and stearamide, added to plastics as slip agents; notorious for appearing in positive mode from pipette tips and bags. More slip agents and fatty amides →
