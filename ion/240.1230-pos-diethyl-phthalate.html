Where it comes from

Plastic labware, tubing, fragrance and cosmetic carryover.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.5, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognizable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

Ammonium adduct in ammonium acetate or formate eluents
