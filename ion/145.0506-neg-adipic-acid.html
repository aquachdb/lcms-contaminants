Where it comes from

Nylon-6,6 hydrolysis, plasticizer hydrolysis, packaging.

Chromatography: void volume to early eluting.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.1, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →

How to recognise it
