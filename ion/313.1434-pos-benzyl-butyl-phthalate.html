Where it comes from

Vinyl flooring, sealants, PVC tubing, conveyor belting.

Other sources report the same ion from: Vinyl flooring, plastic labware, adhesives; Plastic tubing, vials, gloves, caps.

Chromatography: elutes late on reversed phase.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognizable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →
