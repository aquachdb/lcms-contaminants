Where it comes from

DEHP hydrolysis product, PVC leachate, biological samples.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastic tubing, vials, gloves, caps.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.7, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognizable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognize it

  • Preferred negative-mode form; fragments to m/z 134.0373 and 121.0295
