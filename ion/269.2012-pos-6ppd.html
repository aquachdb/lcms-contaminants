Where it comes from

Tire and rubber antiozonant, tubing and seal leachate, road dust.

Other sources report the same ion from: Tire and rubber antiozonant, road dust and ambient air; Also rubber consumables.

Chromatography: elutes late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.6, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →

How to recognize it

  • Strong ESI+ from the diamine; oxidises to 6PPD-quinone
  • ESI+ 269.2012; ubiquitous urban ambient background
