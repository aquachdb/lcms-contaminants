Where it comes from

Rubber accelerator in gloves, stoppers, septa, tubing (also a fungicide).

Other sources report the same ion from: Rubber vulcanization accelerator from stoppers, septa and gloves; Rubber accelerator in gloves and closures, also a fungicide.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.7, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →

How to recognize it

  • Degrades to dimethyldithiocarbamate; distinctive S4 isotope pattern
  • Glove and stopper leachate; characteristic S4 isotope pattern
  • ESI+ 240.9899; degrades to dimethyldithiocarbamate 119.99 in ESI-
