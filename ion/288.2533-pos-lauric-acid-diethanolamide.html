Where it comes from

Shampoo/detergent thickener and foam booster, hand soap residue on glassware, personal care carryover.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hand soap and lab dish detergent residue on glassware; Hand soap, shampoo and cleaner foam booster residue; Shampoo/detergent foam booster, personal care carryover, hand soap residue.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late gradient, later with longer acyl chain.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 3.5, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Ethoxylated surfactants -- Triton, Tween/polysorbate, alcohol and alkylphenol ethoxylates -- which ionise extremely well and appear as broad ethylene-oxide envelopes long after the glassware was washed. More non-ionic surfactants →

How to recognise it

  • Classic soap-residue positive-mode ion; comb of C12 to C18 homologs spaced 28.0313; fragment 106.0863 (diethanolamine + H)
  • ESI+ 288.2533; homolog comb spaced 28.0313; frequent soap carryover
  • Fatty acid alkanolamide homolog series; members differ by CH2 (14.0157) and by 2H (2.0157) for unsaturation. Commercial cocamide DEA is a mixture…
