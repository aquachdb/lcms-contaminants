Where it comes from

Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Other sources report the same ion from: Skin/sebum transfer, waxes.

Chromatography: standardized RT 9.09 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.5, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →
