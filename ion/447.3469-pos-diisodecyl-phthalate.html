Where it comes from

Flexible PVC, wire and cable, gaskets, automotive interiors.

Other sources report the same ion from: PVC plastics, cable insulation, tubing.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 10.6, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Dialkyl phthalates leached from PVC tubing, gloves and plastic labware. Their shared in-source fragment at m/z 149.0233 is one of the most recognisable signals in LC-MS. More phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Branched C10 isomer mixture; broad unresolved envelope; fragment m/z 149.0233 and 307.1904
  • Phthalate ester plasticiser; yields m/z 149 fragment; Diisodecyl phthalate
