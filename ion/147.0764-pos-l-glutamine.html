Where it comes from

Cell culture media at 2-4 mM, the highest-concentration amino acid.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -3.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

Highest-concentration amino acid in media; degrades to glutamate and ammonia
