Where it comes from

Boiler/steam amine, cleaning products, cosmetics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Poly(2-hydroxyethyl aspartamide) HILIC columns (PolyLC PolyHYDROXYETHYL A) after amide hydrolysis.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -1.3, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds that arrive through the laboratory air or water supply rather than through the sample. More ambient and environmental background →

How to recognize it

  • Very poorly retained; ESI+ 62.0600; steam-system amine background
