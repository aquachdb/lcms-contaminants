Where it comes from

Thermal decomposition product of urea in denaturing buffers.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.7, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Derivatization reagents, chaotropes, reducing and alkylating agents, and extraction additives. More sample-preparation reagents →

How to recognize it

Forms when urea solutions are heated; marker of carbamylation-prone urea buffer
