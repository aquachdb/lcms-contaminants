At m/z 267.1720 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of Tributyl phosphate (C12H27O4P, monoisotopic 266.1647).

Where it comes from

Flame retardant/plasticizer in plastics, foams and coatings.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plastics, hydraulic fluids, antifoam, solvent extraction residue; Plastics, hydraulic fluids, antifoam agents; Plastic tubing and filter extractable, antifoam and solvent residue.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.9, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Very common ESI+ background ion; fragments to m/z 155.0206 and 99.0080
  • Isomeric with TBP, elutes slightly earlier
  • Common LC-MS background; fragments 99.0155 and 155.0781
