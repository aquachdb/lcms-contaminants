Where it comes from

RIPA lysis buffer, membrane protein prep, bile salt detergent.

Other sources report the same ion from: Bile salt detergent stocks, biological matrix carryover; Bile salt reagent, biological matrix carryover; Proteomics lysis and phase-transfer digestion (alternative to SDS).

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognize it

  • Classic RIPA buffer carryover ion; water losses 373.27 and 355.26
  • Isobaric with deoxycholic acid; separated chromatographically only
  • Third isobar of the C24H40O4 dihydroxy bile acids
