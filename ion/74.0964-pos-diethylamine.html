Where it comes from

Basic modifier and TEA impurity/degradation product.

Other sources report the same ion from: Ion-pairing/basic additive; Quaternary ammonium ion-pairing reagent and phase-transfer catalyst residue.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.6, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

TFA, HFBA, triethylamine, hexylamine and related reagents, which persist in an LC system for a very long time after the method that used them. More ion-pairing reagents →

How to recognize it

  • Protonated diethylamine; also present as an impurity in triethylamine
  • Protonated butylamine; isomeric with diethylamine
  • Permanent quaternary cation; no protonation needed so it is seen even in pure organic
