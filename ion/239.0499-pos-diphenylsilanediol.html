Where it comes from

Diphenyl and biphenyl-type bonded phases; also a hydrolysis product of phenyl-containing silicone fluids.

Chromatography: elutes mid-gradient.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

Well-known background ion; on a diphenyl column it is genuine phase bleed, but phenylmethyl silicone oils give the same species, so a column-out…
