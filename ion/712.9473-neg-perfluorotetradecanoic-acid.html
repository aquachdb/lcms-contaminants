Where it comes from

LC system PFAS background: PTFE/fluoropolymer tubing, degasser membranes, solvent bottle caps, filters, SPE frits and mobile-phase solvents.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 9.0, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
