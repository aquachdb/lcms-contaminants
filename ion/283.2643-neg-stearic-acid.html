Where it comes from

Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction); Polypropylene disposable pipette tips (Eppendorf/Fisher).

Other sources report the same ion from: Skin/sebum transfer, soaps, lubricants, glove slip agents; Plasma, FBS, skin/glove and plasticware background; Ubiquitous fatty acid from skin/matrix/plastics.

Chromatography: standardized RT 7.02 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized); RT 7.4 min (RP-C18 21.5 min gradient).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • With palmitic acid the dominant FFA blank pair; 283.2643 in ESI-
  • Ubiquitous background fatty acid; also a lubricant/slip additive in plasticware
  • MaConDa theoretical adduct mass 283.2642536
