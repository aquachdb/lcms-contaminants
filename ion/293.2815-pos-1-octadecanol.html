At m/z 293.2815 in positive ion mode, this is [M+Na]+ of 1-Octadecanol (C18H38O, monoisotopic 270.2923).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Personal-care and cosmetic residues.
Open m/z 293.2815 in the finder →

Where it comes from

Hydrolysis of carbamate-embedded C18 phases (releases CO2 plus the fatty alcohol); also a general lipid background.

Other sources report the same ion from: Hand cream and conditioner transfer.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

  • Poor ESI response; sodiated adduct
  • Terminal breakdown product of a carbamate-embedded ligand; protonates very poorly so it is seen as the sodium or ammonium adduct or not at all
