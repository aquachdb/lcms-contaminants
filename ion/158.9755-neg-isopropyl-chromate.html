Where it comes from

Cr(VI) released by oxidative corrosion of stainless steel 316 flow-path components (frits, fittings, passivated tubing), esterified in situ by the mobile-phase alcohol.

Chromatography: maximises in the isopropanol-rich part of the gradient.

Compounds from PEEK, PTFE, silicone and elastomer seals in the fluid path. More tubing and seal materials →
