Where it comes from

Membrane protein solubilization buffers, 2D gel and IEF sample buffers.

Other sources report the same ion from: Membrane protein solubilisation, IEF/2-D sample buffers; Solvent; Zwitterionic detergent in protein sample prep.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.9, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Anionic and cationic detergents: alkyl sulfates and sulfonates, quaternary ammonium compounds, bile-salt and zwitterionic detergents used in sample prep. More ionic surfactants and detergents →

How to recognise it

  • Zwitterionic bile-acid derived detergent; strong ion suppressor; also seen as [M+Na]+ and in negative mode; remove by SPE or precipitation
  • Zwitterionic detergent; extremely persistent in LC systems; also gives a CHAPSO analogue at +15.995
