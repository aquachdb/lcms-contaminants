Where it comes from

Legacy sunscreen UV filter, vitamin supplement.

Chromatography: early eluting.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.8, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Parabens, UV filters, fragrance components and emollients transferred from skin, hand cream and hair products through gloves and glassware. More personal-care and cosmetic residues →
