Where it comes from

LC system PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottle caps and SPE consumables; short-chain PFAS target of EPA Method 533.

Other sources report the same ion from: Fluoropolymer tubing, PTFE frits and seals, solvent line extractables; Strong ion-pairing modifier for very polar basic analytes; PFAS background from PTFE/fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottles, LC seals and reagents.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.2, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • Very polar PFAS background; CF2 comb spaced 49.9968
  • Heptafluorobutyrate; far stronger retention modifier and stronger suppressor than TFA; near-permanent system carryover once used
  • ESI- only; fragment 168.99 by CO2 loss; mitigate with a PFAS delay column between pump and injector; homolog comb spaced CF2 49.9968
