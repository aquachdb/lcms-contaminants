Where it comes from

Extractables & leachables from container-closure systems, stoppers, syringes and single-use bioprocess plastics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Nylon filters, frits, tubing, packaging films; Nylon syringe filters, membranes and frits.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP -0.1, very early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.
