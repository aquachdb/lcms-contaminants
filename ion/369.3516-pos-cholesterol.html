Where it comes from

Skin and sebum transfer, serum carryover, dust.

Other sources report the same ion from: Plasma, serum, FBS in media, cell membranes; Cholesterol-bonded phases (Cosmosil Cholester, Ascentis Cholesterol); In-source dehydration of the released cholesteryl group.

Chromatography: elutes late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 8.7, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Lipids, amino acids, sugars and other endogenous species so common they act as background in almost any biological matrix. More ubiquitous matrix components →

How to recognize it

  • ESI+ almost always as water-loss ion 369.3516; ammoniated 404.3887 with ammonium acetate; APCI+ preferred
  • Cholesterol ionises poorly; the observed ion is almost always the water-loss species at 369.3516, best in APCI
  • The dominant observed ion from any cholesteryl species; 369.3516 with a large positive mass defect. On a cholesterol column it rises with column age…
