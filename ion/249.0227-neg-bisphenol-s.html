Where it comes from

Polycarbonate/epoxy and thermal-paper leachate.

Other sources report the same ion from: BPA-free thermal paper, epoxy coatings, can linings; BPA-free plastics and thermal paper carryover.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.9, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →
