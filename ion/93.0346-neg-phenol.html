Where it comes from

Phenolic resin residues, TRIzol/phenol extraction carryover, plastics.

Chromatography: elutes early-mid.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 1.5, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognize it

From phenol-chloroform extraction carryover and from phenolic polymer residues; strong negative ion
