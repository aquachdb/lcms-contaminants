Where it comes from

UV-curable inks, coatings, adhesives, printed packaging.

Chromatography: elutes mid to late.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 2.6, mid — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Cyclic and linear oligomers of nylon, PET, polycaprolactone, PVP and acrylates, shed by containers, filters and membranes. More other polymer oligomers →
