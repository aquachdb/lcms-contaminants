Where it comes from

Cyclodextrin-bonded chiral and polar phases (Astec Cyclobond I, ChiraDex, Nucleodex beta-OH); also a mobile-phase additive and formulation excipient.

Chromatography: retained in HILIC segment; void volume on reversed phase.

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →

How to recognise it

The most common bonded cyclodextrin; series spacing to the alpha and gamma homologues is 162.0528 (one glucose unit), which is the definitive check
