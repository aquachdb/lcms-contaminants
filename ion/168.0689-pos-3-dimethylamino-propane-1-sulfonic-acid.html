Where it comes from

Zwitterionic sulfoalkylbetaine HILIC phases: SeQuant ZIC-HILIC, ZIC-pHILIC, Waters Atlantis BEH Z-HILIC, Merck ZIC-HILIC guard cartridges.

Chromatography: retained and elutes mid-gradient in the HILIC segment (approx 10 min).

Silanols, bonded-phase ligands and siloxanes released by the column itself, which rise with column age and with pH extremes. More column and stationary-phase bleed →
