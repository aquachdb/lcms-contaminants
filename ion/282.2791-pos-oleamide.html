Where it comes from

Eppendorf polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes (blank Folch lipid extraction).

Other sources report the same ion from: Slip agent leached from polypropylene tubes, pipette tips, plates and syringes; Slip agent from plastic bags, gloves and packaging handled by hand; Slip agent.

Chromatography: standardized RT 5.53 min (RP-C18, Splash Lipidomix-standardized).

Lipophilicity: XLogP 6.6, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Oleamide, erucamide and stearamide, added to plastics as slip agents; notorious for appearing in positive mode from pipette tips and bags. More slip agents and fatty amides →
