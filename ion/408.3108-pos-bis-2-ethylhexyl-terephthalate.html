Where it comes from

Non-phthalate PVC plasticizer in tubing, medical devices, packaging.

Other sources report the same ion from: PVC tubing and blood bags, plastic vials, gloves, syringe filters; PVC articles, tubing, cable insulation.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 7.4, very late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Adipates, citrates, sebacates, trimellitates and phosphate esters used as phthalate replacements; same sources, different masses. More non-phthalate plasticizers →

How to recognise it

  • Dominant DEHP ion in ammonium acetate/formate eluents; fragments cleanly to 279.1591 and 149.0233
  • Ammonium adduct; fragment m/z 149.0233
  • Isomeric with DEHP but elutes differently and gives m/z 149.0233 only weakly; para fragment m/z 261.1485 dominates
