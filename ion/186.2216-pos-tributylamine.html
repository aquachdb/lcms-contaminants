Where it comes from

Ion-pairing reagent for nucleotides and organic acids in negative-mode metabolomics.

Other sources report the same ion from: Fatty amine antistat and corrosion inhibitor residue, quat synthesis impurity; Quaternary ammonium ion-pairing reagent; Solvent.

Chromatography: ubiquitous across gradient.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.0, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

TFA, HFBA, triethylamine, hexylamine and related reagents, which persist in an LC system for a very long time after the method that used them. More ion-pairing reagents →

How to recognize it

  • Primary fatty amine comb spaced 14.0157; very efficient ESI+ ionizer
  • Protonated tributylamine; contaminates an instrument semi-permanently - dedicate a column and ideally an instrument to TBA methods
  • Permanent quaternary cation; isobaric with protonated tributylamine
