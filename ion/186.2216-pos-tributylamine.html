At m/z 186.2216 in positive ion mode, this is [M+H]+ of Tributylamine (C12H27N, monoisotopic 185.2143).

  • This m/z is recomputed here from the molecular formula and the adduct, electron mass included.
  • Sources disagree on how to classify it — it is also reported as Ionic surfactants and detergents, Ion-pairing reagents, Solvent and mobile-phase impurities.
  • Accurate mass alone is not diagnostic here: other known chemical structures — not only the ones in this table — sit within a few ppm of it. Use retention time, the isotope pattern or MS2 to choose.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.0, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it

  • Primary fatty amine comb spaced 14.0157; very efficient ESI+ ionizer
  • Protonated tributylamine; contaminates an instrument semi-permanently - dedicate a column and ideally an instrument to TBA methods
  • Permanent quaternary cation; isobaric with protonated tributylamine
