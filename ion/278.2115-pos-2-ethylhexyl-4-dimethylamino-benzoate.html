Where it comes from

Amine synergist in UV printing inks, packaging migrant, also a UV filter.

Other sources report the same ion from: Sunscreen UV filter transfer.

Chromatography: elutes very late/lipophilic.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 5.0, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Compounds leached from tubes, plates, tips, filters, membranes and syringes, which is the class the literature most often traces a mystery peak back to. More labware leachables →

How to recognise it

  • Strong ESI+ from the dimethylamino group; fragments to m/z 166.0863 and 151.0628
  • ESI+ 278.2115 with dominant fragment 166.0863 (dimethylaminobenzoic acid)
