Where it comes from

Extractable/leachable from single-use polymeric films and bioprocess bags (LC-HRMS E&L screening).

Other sources report the same ion from: Hydrolysis/degradation product of Irgafos 168 and phosphite antioxidants, plastic labware; Degradation product of octylphenol ethoxylates (Triton X), plastic and rubber additive; Degradation product of Triton X-100 and X-114, plastics leachate.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 4.9, late — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

Hindered-phenol and phosphite antioxidants (Irganox, Irgafos) and UV stabilisers that migrate out of plastic containers and tubing. More polymer antioxidants and stabilisers →

How to recognise it

  • Extremely common plasticware background, frequently misidentified as a natural product
  • Isobaric with deprotonated 2,4-di-tert-butylphenol; separate chromatographically
  • Parent phenol of the Triton X ethoxamer comb; sharp single peak unlike nonylphenol
