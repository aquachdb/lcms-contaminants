Where it comes from

Ammonium fluoride additive; glass etching.

Other sources report the same ion from: Phosphatase inhibitor, glycolysis inhibitor in grey-top blood tubes.

Chromatography: void volume.

Lipophilicity: XLogP 0.6, early — a rough reversed-phase retention proxy, not a prediction.

How to recognise it

  • Fluoride anion; below most scan ranges
  • Fluoride is below almost every LC-MS scan range; its practical signature is fluoride/oxalate tube plasma rather than a direct ion
