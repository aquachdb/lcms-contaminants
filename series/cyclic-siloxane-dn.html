26 ionsm/z 223.0637 – 779.1511repeat C2H6OSi (74.0188 Da)

A homologous series is recognised by its spacing, not by any single mass: consecutive members differ by one repeat unit, here C2H6OSi (74.0188 Da). Seeing that regular comb in a blank identifies the whole envelope at once.
