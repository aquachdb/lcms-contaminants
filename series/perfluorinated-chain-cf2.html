Where the series comes from

  • LC system PFAS background from fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottle caps and SPE consumables; short-chain PFAS target of EPA Method 533 (9 ions)
  • LC system PFAS background: PTFE/fluoropolymer tubing, degasser membranes, solvent bottle caps, filters, SPE frits and mobile-phase solvents (5 ions)
  • PFAS background from PTFE/fluoropolymer tubing, degassers, solvent bottles, LC seals and reagents (4 ions)
  • Perfluoropolymer (PTFE/PFA/FEP) seals, tubing and fittings (1 ion)
  • PFAS background from fluoropolymer and coating residues (1 ion)
  • PFAS background at high concentration in fluoropolymer-rich systems (1 ion)
