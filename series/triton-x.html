Where the series comes from

  • Hydrogenated nonionic detergent used for UV-transparent buffers (76 ions)
  • Nonionic detergent for cell lysis and membrane protein solubilisation (60 ions)
  • Triton X-100 and X-114 detergents, cell lysis and membrane protein buffers, laboratory glassware detergent (42 ions)
  • Nonionic detergent used in cell lysis buffers (41 ions)
  • Nonionic detergent, cell lysis buffers and labware residue (35 ions)
  • Nonionic detergent, protein solubilisation and labware residue (12 ions)
